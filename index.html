Privacy Policy

Bluetooth Brain, published under the name Asterisk Computing

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Summary: Your profiles and settings stay on your phone; we run no servers and never receive them. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information for those ads as described below. Premium users see no ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

Who is responsible

Bluetooth Brain ("the App") is published on Google Play under the name Asterisk Computing. Asterisk Computing is a name, not a company. The person responsible for the App and for this policy is Utku Yıldız ("we", "us"). You can reach us at asteriskcomputing@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your phone

The App stores the following only on your phone, in its private storage:

  • Your device profiles: the names and Bluetooth addresses of the devices you choose, and the volume, brightness and night-schedule settings you set for them
  • The settings each profile changed, so the App can put them back when the device disconnects
  • App preferences, such as language and theme

We do not operate any servers and never receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an ad when you open the App and a banner ad. To show and measure these ads, Google's advertising software inside the App may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), basic device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. This information goes to Google, not to us. Google describes how it uses it here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App shows Google's consent message before personalised ads are used, and you can change your choice later from the privacy options in the App's settings. That option appears only where it is required. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Premium users see no ads.

Purchases

Premium and other in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The App only receives confirmation from Google Play of what you have bought, so it can unlock Premium. Google's handling of purchases is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Advertising the App

We advertise the App through Google Ads. Google may measure whether an install or a purchase followed one of those ads, using information from Google Play. We only see totals, such as the number of installs, and cannot identify you from them.

If you contact us

If you email us, including through the App's feedback or problem-report options, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems with the App, and never for anything else.

Your choices and rights

You can delete all data the App stores by uninstalling it, change your ad consent from the App's settings, and reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings. To ask, email us at the address above.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
